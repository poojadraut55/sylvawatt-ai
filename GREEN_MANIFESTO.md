# 🌱 SylvaWatt Green Movement & Human-Control Manifesto

**Owned by DevOps Invent Private Limited**

## 1. Save Fresh Water From the AI Data Center Boom
Every megawatt inside a centralized hyper-scale AI facility triggers massive evaporative water consumption — competing directly with agriculture, rivers, and drinking water supplies. By shifting distributed inference and micro-batches onto millions of idle consumer workstations already cooled ambiently at home, humanity gets AI without draining Earth's aquifers.

## 2. Keep Control Strictly With Humans — Never Surrender to Autonomous AI
Technology must serve human sovereignty, never silently hijack user hardware:
- **Explicit Thermal & Load Ceilings:** No workload can ever override the human's maximum GPU % or °C temperature threshold.
- **Instant Yield:** Gaming, video calls, or creative work always take instant priority.
- **Full Auditability:** Every container job executes with cryptographic checksums and transparent LAN isolation logs.

## 3. Fair Rewards, Not Trillion-Dollar Store Coupons
Home PC owners invest thousands of dollars into GPUs, memory, and electricity. SylvaWatt guarantees an **88% provider share** on compute jobs, transparent post-electricity **Net Profit math**, and direct tools to scale hardware wisely.
